# Worker productivity and quality of life among adults with T2DM

This is a respository for a project at UCSD's DIMM clinic. 

Proposed title of the project: "*Impact of SGLT2 and GLP1RA Use on Productivity Loss and Veterans Rand-12 Health Survey (VR-12) Physical and Mental Health in U.S. Adults with Type 2 Diabetes using pooled data from the Medical Expenditure Panel Survey (2018 to 2022)*"

## Specific Aims
### Primary Aim: To evaluate the impact of SGLT2i / GLP1-RA on productivity and quality of life among US adults with type 2 diabetes in the US between 2018 and 2022.
**Primary research question:** Do respondents with type 2 diabetes with and without SGLT2/GLP1RA have similar productivity (lost days) and quality of life (VR-12 physical and mental health components) in the representative sample of the U.S. adult population over 5 years (2018-2022)? 

### Secondary Aim: To evaluate the trends in productivity and quality of life among US adults with type 2 diabetes and receiving SGLT2i / GLP1-RA from 2018 to 2022.
**Secondary research question:** Do respondents with type 2 diabetes with and without SGLT2/GLP1RA have similar trends in productivity (lost days) and quality of life (VR-12 physical and mental health components) in the representative sample of the U.S. adult population between 2018 and 2022?
